# Customer Onboarding Playbook

New enterprise customers move through four onboarding phases: discovery, data mapping, pilot launch, and production rollout.

During discovery, the customer success team identifies business goals, compliance requirements, core users, and the first two workflows that should be automated.

During data mapping, the implementation engineer reviews source systems, access permissions, document formats, and retention requirements. Customers must confirm who owns each data source before ingestion begins.

The pilot launch should include no more than 25 users. The recommended pilot window is two weeks. Success is measured by answer accuracy, citation quality, time saved, and user adoption.

Production rollout begins after the pilot owner approves retrieval quality. The rollout plan must include admin training, support escalation paths, and a review of audit logging.

If the knowledge base does not contain enough context to answer a user question, the assistant should say that the information is not available in the provided sources.
